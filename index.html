I am a member of the CSDL Lab, supervised by Prof. Seokhyeong Kang, in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH). Since July 2026, I am currently an on-site Visiting Graduate Scholar with the ABKGroup (VLSI CAD Laboratory) at the University of California San Diego, hosted by Prof. Andrew B. Kahng. My research focuses on differentiable optimization frameworks for VLSI CAD objectives and AI-driven approaches to electronic design automation. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering and M.S. from the Graduate School of AI at POSTECH. During my M.S., I was supervised by Prof. Jaesik Park in the Computer Vision Lab.
